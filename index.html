Why I picked this for LivingAI

PashuRaksha already does herd health and vet alerts, PashuMandi does price discovery, and your NLP covers 22 languages. A farmer can use all of that just by calling, so I wanted to build that one call.

What 1 call covers

  • His own cow records, like milk per cow, pregnancy, vaccination due and medical history
  • General cattle questions about breeds, breeding age and pregnancy period
  • Preliminary health guidance, and it tells him to call a vet when it looks like an emergency
  • What his cow might sell for, from past sales of similar cows

I already built this flow once, for banking

My banking voice agent answers a bank customer in Telugu, Hindi, English or Hinglish. I built the router as a LangGraph state machine with Groq gpt-oss-120b, Postgres on Neon for account data, ChromaDB for policy RAG and Vachana by Gnani.ai for Indian voice TTS. I ran 95 labelled cases through the real pipeline, nothing mocked.
